# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat people
with respect, give constructive feedback about the work, and assume good intent
while remaining open to correction.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
doxxing and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.
These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project
spaces, including interactions where someone represents the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close abusive discussions,
or restrict participation. Responses should be proportional to the behavior;
serious threats or repeated misconduct can result in a permanent restriction.

For abuse or sensitive incidents, use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report
abuse** feature on the relevant content or profile. Do not publish sensitive
details in a public issue. Maintainers can address behavior in repository spaces;
GitHub handles reports made through its reporting tools. For ordinary moderation
requests without sensitive details, contact maintainers in the relevant discussion.
